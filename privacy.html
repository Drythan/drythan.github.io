Privacy policy

rclone-backup is a personal backup tool used by its owner only.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive of the account that grants it access.
  • It writes and reads encrypted backup files in that Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, store or share any personal data with third parties.
  • No data leaves the owner's own systems and the owner's own Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings under Security, third-party apps.
